Etiquette of Dua

A quick reminder, for before you begin

  1. Begin and end with praise of Allah and salawat on the Prophet ﷺ Tirmidhi, sahih
  2. Ask with certainty of an answer, not half-heartedly Bukhari & Muslim
  3. Be specific — ask by name and in detail, as the Sahaba did Sunnah
  4. Supplicate often in ease, not only in hardship Tirmidhi
  5. The last third of the night and right after fard prayer are especially accepted times Tirmidhi
  6. Don't rush or grow impatient and give up Bukhari & Muslim
  7. Bring a present, attentive heart Tirmidhi, hasan
  8. Call Him by the specific Name that fits your need Qur'an 7:180

The Dua That Calls Every Name

For when you don't know which Name to reach for

...أَسْأَلُكَ بِكُلِّ اسْمٍ هُوَ لَكَ، سَمَّيْتَ بِهِ نَفْسَكَ، أَوْ أَنْزَلْتَهُ فِي كِتَابِكَ، أَوْ عَلَّمْتَهُ أَحَدًا مِنْ خَلْقِكَ، أَوِ اسْتَأْثَرْتَ بِهِ فِي عِلْمِ الْغَيْبِ عِنْدَكَ

...as'aluka bikulli ismin huwa laka, sammayta bihi nafsak, aw anzaltahu fi kitabik, aw 'allamtahu ahadan min khalqik, awista'tharta bihi fi 'ilmil-ghaybi 'indak

...I ask You by every Name that is Yours — that You named Yourself with, or revealed in Your Book, or taught to any of Your creation, or kept in the knowledge of the unseen with You.

Musnad Ahmad, sahih (al-Albani) — narrated by Ibn Mas'ud
